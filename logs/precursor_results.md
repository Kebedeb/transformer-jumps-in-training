## 8. Implementation choices and ambiguities

1. t50 is computed with `stays_above_step` from checkpoints_analysis/jump_detection.py on metrics.csv and checked against the per-run table of logs/run_results.md before any signal is used; all matched.
2. L = `model.n_layers` from each run's config.json, so the final residual is induction: L = [2] → `resid2_*`; icl: L = [2] → `resid2_*`; grokking: L = [1] → `resid1_*`.
3. Signal 5 averages, and signal 6 takes the max over, every column matching `attn_L<digits>H<digits>_entropy` / `_prev_token`, i.e. all heads in all layers: induction: 8 entropy, 8 prev_token columns; icl: 8 entropy, 8 prev_token columns; grokking: 4 entropy, 4 prev_token columns.
4. Signal 4 drops only the step-0 row (where `cka_prev` is NaN by construction); its series starts at the second checkpoint. Any other NaN would be kept and listed in section 9.
5. Smoothing is a centered rolling median over 5 checkpoints with min_periods=1 (windows are truncated at the series ends; NaNs are skipped). Centering means each smoothed value uses the next 2 checkpoints. The pre-registration says only “rolling median over 5 checkpoints”; centering was specified in the analysis instructions.
6. Reference window = checkpoints with step in [0.05·t50, 0.30·t50], both ends inclusive, evaluated on the smoothed series. m and MAD are taken over the smoothed values in this window. sigma = max(1.4826·MAD, 1e-6·|m| + 1e-12) (“floor” read as a lower bound).
7. “Outside the band” means strictly below m − 3σ or strictly above m + 3σ; a value exactly on the band edge counts as inside. A NaN smoothed value would count as not outside.
8. Onset candidates are checkpoints with 0.30·t50 < step ≤ t50. The onset is the earliest candidate from which every checkpoint up to and including t50 is outside the band. The pre-registration says no onset if the signal is inside the band “just before t50”; the analysis instructions say “at t50”. The literal instruction (at t50) is implemented. Because qualifying requires lead ≥ 2 checkpoint intervals, any run with an onset that qualifies is also outside the band at the checkpoint just before t50, so the two readings give the same qualification results.
9. Direction = sign(smoothed value at t50 − m), recorded for every run including those without an onset; precursor direction uses qualifying seeds only.
10. Checkpoint interval = median step spacing of signals.csv (spacings are listed in section 9 if not uniform). Minimum lead = max(2·interval, 0.05·t50); lead equal to the minimum qualifies.
11. Precursor: n_qualifying ≥ n_seeds − 1 and the qualifying seeds have one direction. The pre-registration's “direction agrees” is read as agreement among qualifying seeds.
12. Shared candidate requires a precursor in all three tasks with one common direction and max/min of the three median normalized leads ≤ 3. The pre-registered specificity condition (controls) is not evaluated, so no overall Supports/Partial/Refutes verdict is given. “One seed short” and the S2 trigger are reported as facts only.
13. The positive control uses the same onset rule, and qualifies under the same minimum-lead rule.
14. Spearman ρ uses average ranks for ties and is computed only over seeds with an onset.
15. Only the pre-registered defaults were run. No other signals, thresholds or parameters were tried.
